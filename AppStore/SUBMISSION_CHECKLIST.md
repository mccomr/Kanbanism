# App Store submission checklist

The Xcode project contains the app icon, privacy manifest, version/build numbers, distribution identity, and Mac Catalyst support.

Complete these account and publishing steps before submission:

- Register `com.kanbanetti.app` in the Apple Developer portal.
- Confirm the existing development team owns that App ID and refresh signing in Xcode.
- Create the Kanbanetti app record in App Store Connect.
- Publish support and privacy pages at the URLs in `METADATA.md`.
- Capture current iPhone, iPad, and Mac screenshots from a release build.
- Complete App Privacy as “Data Not Collected.”
- Answer export compliance as no encryption beyond Apple operating-system services.
- Set pricing, availability, age rating, and app review contact details.
- Archive with the `Kanbanetti` scheme, validate the archive, then upload it to App Store Connect.
