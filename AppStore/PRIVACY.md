# Kanbanetti privacy policy

Kanbanetti does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Projects, opportunities, and tasks are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, accounts, or cloud synchronization.

Users can remove their stored data by deleting projects in the app or uninstalling Kanbanetti.

For privacy questions, publish a contact address on `https://kanbanetti.app/privacy` before App Store submission.
