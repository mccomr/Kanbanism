## Description

Kanbanetti is a visual Kanban board built for work that begins with an opportunity, partner, or vendor and continues through connected tasks.

Create boards and workflow columns, assign bright colors to each lead, and see related tasks inherit the same color. Curved connectors keep relationships visible as cards move across the board. Reorder and stack cards, focus on one lead at a time, and give each column a soft color that suits your workflow.

Kanbanetti stores boards locally on your device. No account is required.

Kanbanetti does not require an account or network connection. Create a project, add a lead in the first column, then add linked tasks in subsequent columns. All data is stored locally in Application Support.
